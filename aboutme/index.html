About me

Hi! I’m a versatile Roblox developer with over six years of experience creating, designing, and contributing to immersive digital projects. My creative journey actually began in 2017, when I started exploring video editing at a young age. What began as simple editing experiments quickly grew into a passion for digital creation.

In 2019, when I was 9 years old, I entered the world of Roblox development. From there, my skills expanded rapidly—first into animation, then GFX design, building, and photo editing. Over time, I discovered how much I enjoy blending creativity with technical problem-solving, and I’m now actively learning scripting to bring complete game ideas to life.

English is my first language, and I love collaborating, learning from others, and constantly challenging myself to improve.

My Growth & Milestones

I’m proud of the progress I’ve made throughout the years:

  • 2020: Reached 10,000+ contributed visits, 720 favorites, and 2,000+ social media views.

  • 2023: Grew to 326,000+ contributed visits and 34,000+ social media views.

  • 2025: Now at 531,000+ contributed visits and over 70,000+ social platform views.

These milestones highlight my dedication and my commitment to creating quality experiences that players enjoy.

Collaboration Experience

I’ve worked with 19 different studios, both solo and as part of a team. Each project has helped me refine my skills, learn new techniques, and understand what makes a development environment successful.
